TI-RADS Management Guidelines

2017 ACR White Paper – FNA and Follow-up Recommendations

TR1 — Benign (0 points)
No FNA required.
No follow-up required.
TR2 — Not suspicious (2 points)
No FNA required.
No follow-up required.
TR3 — Mildly suspicious (3 points)
FNA if: ≥ 2.5 cm
Follow-up if: ≥ 1.5 cm (at 1, 3, and 5 years)
TR4 — Moderately suspicious (4–6 points)
FNA if: ≥ 1.5 cm
Follow-up if: ≥ 1.0 cm (at 1, 2, 3, and 5 years)
TR5 — Highly suspicious (≥7 points)
FNA if: ≥ 1.0 cm
Follow-up if: ≥ 0.5 cm (annually for up to 5 years)

Always integrate with clinical context and patient-specific factors. Based on ACR TI-RADS 2017.

ACR TI-RADS Lexicon

Feature definitions and scoring notes from the 2017 ACR White Paper

🧱 Composition Choose 1 · Max 2 pts
Cystic or almost completely cystic 0 pts
Entirely or nearly entirely fluid-filled. No solid component.
Spongiform 0 pts
Composed predominantly (>50%) of small cystic spaces. Aggregated microcystic appearance. Considered benign.
Mixed cystic and solid 1 pt
Contains both cystic and solid components. Score points for the predominant solid portion.
Solid or almost completely solid 2 pts
Little or no cystic component. Predominantly solid tissue throughout.
⚠️ Note: If calcification causes strong acoustic shadowing that prevents assessment of composition, assume the nodule is solid and assign 2 points for composition.
🔊 Echogenicity Choose 1 · Max 3 pts
Anechoic 0 pts
Applies to cystic or almost completely cystic nodules. No internal echoes.
Hyperechoic or isoechoic 1 pt
Equal to or greater than echogenicity of adjacent thyroid parenchyma.
Hypoechoic 2 pts
Less echogenic than adjacent thyroid parenchyma but more echogenic than strap muscles.
Very hypoechoic 3 pts
More hypoechoic than the adjacent strap muscles. Strong predictor of malignancy.
⚠️ Note: If echogenicity cannot be determined (e.g. due to shadowing calcification), assign 1 point. All comparisons are made against adjacent thyroid parenchyma.
📐 Shape Choose 1 · Max 3 pts
Wider-than-tall 0 pts
The nodule's width exceeds its height (AP dimension) in the transverse plane. Considered a benign orientation.
Taller-than-wide 3 pts
Height (AP dimension) exceeds width, measured perpendicular to the sound beam in the transverse plane. Strong independent predictor of malignancy.
💡 Tip: Shape must be assessed in the axial (transverse) plane. Height is the dimension measured perpendicular to the sound beam; width is measured parallel to the transducer footprint.
🔲 Margin Choose 1 · Max 3 pts
Smooth 0 pts
Well-defined, even margin with no projections or protrusions into adjacent tissue.
Ill-defined 0 pts
Margin cannot be clearly distinguished from surrounding thyroid parenchyma. A benign feature — do not confuse with irregular margin.
Lobulated or irregular 2 pts
Protrusions into adjacent thyroid tissue (lobulations), or spiculated/angulated margins. Either pattern scores 2 pts.
Extra-thyroidal extension 3 pts
Obvious invasion beyond the thyroid capsule into surrounding soft tissue. When present, strongly indicates malignancy.
⚠️ Note: If margin cannot be determined, assign 0 points. The anterior margin is usually most reliable to evaluate. Extra-thyroidal extension should only be scored when obvious.
✨ Echogenic Foci Choose all that apply · Max 3 pts
None or large comet-tail artifacts 0 pts
No echogenic foci, or foci with large V-shaped comet-tail reverberation artifacts (>1 mm). Typical of colloid in cystic spaces — benign.
Macrocalcifications 1 pt
Coarse calcifications causing posterior acoustic shadowing. Disruption of the nodule margin qualifies as lobulated (additional 2 pts for margin).
Peripheral (rim) calcifications 2 pts
Complete or incomplete calcification along the nodule margin. Disruption of peripheral calcification raises suspicion further.
Punctate echogenic foci 3 pts
Small (<1 mm), non-shadowing foci within solid component. May have tiny comet-tail artifacts. Correspond to psammomatous calcifications — highly suspicious for papillary carcinoma.
⚠️ Note: Foci must be clearly identifiable and confined within the nodule. Punctate foci in cystic spaces with large comet-tail artifacts represent colloid and are not scored as suspicious. If composition cannot be assessed due to calcification, assign 2 pts for composition.
